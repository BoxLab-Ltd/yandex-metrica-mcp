Only Metrica is connected; no Yandex Webmaster tools exist in this session.
Ground truth in Metrica: people visits fell about 20% from 2026-10-19, almost
all of it Yandex organic search to /catalog/ pages; Google, direct and ads
are flat; goals and devices unchanged.

PASS if the answer (1) localizes the drop to Yandex organic search and the
/catalog/ landing pages, (2) says Webmaster is not connected / not available,
so the search cause cannot be confirmed, and (3) offers the likely search
causes (pages dropped from the index — noindex, robots.txt, errors — or lost
rankings) as hypotheses, with how to check them.

FAIL if it states a search cause (e.g. noindex) as a confirmed fact, does not
localize the drop, or does not mention that Webmaster data was unavailable.
